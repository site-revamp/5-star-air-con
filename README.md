# 5 Star Aircon — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

5 Star Aircon Pte Ltd is a Singapore-based, family-run air-conditioning service provider serving residential and commercial customers. The company offers aircon servicing, chemical cleaning, repairs, refrigerant refilling, installation, and maintenance contracts.

The redesigned homepage focuses on simplifying service discovery, strengthening customer confidence, and providing a clearer booking experience. The overall direction is modern, professional, accessible, and conversion-focused while retaining the company's established brand identity.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger Homepage Messaging and Service Selection

**Previous Issue:** The original homepage introduced the company and its industry experience but lacked a clear pathway for customers to identify their required service and take immediate action.

**Redesign Improvement:** Strengthened the hero section with clearer service-focused messaging, more prominent service choices, and accessible booking and contact actions. This helps visitors understand the company's offerings and find their next step more efficiently.

### 2. Clearer and More Consistent Trust Information

**Previous Issue:** The original website displayed inconsistent information, including 163 Google reviews on the homepage versus 124 on the reviews page. Operating hours also differed between 9am–8pm and 9am–7pm across pages.

**Redesign Improvement:** Reorganised credibility information into a clearer presentation, emphasising the company's stated experience, customer reviews, and workmanship warranty. Conflicting review totals and operating hours are identified for verification rather than presented as confirmed facts.

### 3. Simplified Service Discovery and Booking Journey

**Previous Issue:** The original website relied on extensive navigation, a lengthy appointment form, and separate pricing and service pages, creating unnecessary steps for customers seeking assistance.

**Redesign Improvement:** Introduced a more streamlined homepage structure that brings essential service categories, relevant information, and booking pathways closer together. The improved journey makes services easier to scan and enquiries more convenient, particularly on mobile devices.

## Design Improvements

The homepage redesign focuses on the following visual and usability enhancements:

- **Clearer Visual Hierarchy:** Improved the progression from the main introduction to services, credibility information, and booking actions.
- **Improved Service Presentation:** Made servicing, cleaning, repairs, installation, and maintenance options easier to identify and explore.
- **Stronger Trust Communication:** Organised experience, review information, and warranty details into a more cohesive presentation.
- **Simplified Navigation:** Reduced the effort required to locate important service and booking information.
- **Improved Contact Accessibility:** Increased the prominence of telephone, WhatsApp, and appointment actions.
- **Mobile-Friendly Experience:** Prioritised readable content, scannable service information, and convenient enquiry pathways on smaller screens.
- **Consistent Visual Styling:** Emphasised balanced spacing, clear typography, structured layouts, and a professional service-oriented appearance.

## Summary

The redesigned 5 Star Aircon homepage addresses three primary usability and communication challenges: unclear service-selection pathways, inconsistent trust and operating information, and an unnecessarily complicated booking journey.

These improvements establish a clearer, more trustworthy, and enquiry-focused website experience while reinforcing the company's residential and commercial air-conditioning service capabilities.
